Safe Food Rescue Protocol

  • Hot Food: Must be maintained above 60°C or cooled within 2 hours to 5°C.
  • Chilled Items: Keep between 0°C - 5°C to preserve shelf-life for evening distribution.
  • Packaging: Use clean, food-grade food trays, clean containers, or sealed liners.
  • Expiry Time: Never donate food that has exceeded its safe sensory or bacterial lifespan.
